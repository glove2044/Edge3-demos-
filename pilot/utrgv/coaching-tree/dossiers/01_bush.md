**Archetype read**
- Bush's best offensive players are pass-game producers: a 6-7 WR (White), a 17-TD All-American WR (Luke), a 100-ypg WR (Greenberry), a conference OPOY QB (George), a freshman 3,100-yd QB (O'Korn) and a 31-TD transfer QB (Marburger). Volume passing production, not size at RB, is the recurring signal.
- QB profile skews toward accurate, moderately mobile passers who can run when needed (George 63%, Soza 108 rush yds in a game, Ward converted from WR with Wildcat usage, Marburger 4 rush TD); the one classic drop-back in the set (O'Korn) is the one who was benched.
- Recruiting pedigree is mixed: Greenberry was a national (Notre Dame) recruit; Luke, George, Karrington Bush, Soza and Marburger were FCS/G5-level or transfer pieces. Bush's units have produced honors from lightly recruited Texans as often as from blue-chips.
- Home state: nearly every named player is a Texas HS product or Texas-college transfer (Luke, George, Sims, Ward, Farrow, Adams, Marburger; Greenberry from California is the exception); UTRGV 2025 continued this with Texas freshmen earning All-American nods.
- Origin: Texas State and Houston stars were HS-direct developmental players who peaked as sophomores/juniors; UTRGV's first-year stars were a portal QB plus true freshmen, suggesting Bush will pair an experienced transfer QB with HS skill talent.
- Where the pattern broke: the two documented "lows" are QBs in year two of a system (Piland 2012, O'Korn 2014), both losing jobs to more mobile replacements (Jones, Ward). Projection risk for Bush is highest at pocket QB after a staff/scheme change.

**What Bush's own tree says about his HC archetype**
- Texas State (Bailiff/Wright, 2004–09): scheme listed as "Multiple," but Bush's own co-OC units ranked 4th in FCS scoring (2008) and 8th in pass offense (2009); the rewarded profile was a big, record-setting WR (Luke) and an efficient multi-year QB (George). https://goutsa.com/staff/travis-bush ; https://txst.com/news/2008/12/17/121708aaa_632
- UTSA (Coker, 2011): start-up spread with a sophomore QB who ran and threw (Soza 237/108 in game one); a start-from-scratch blueprint he explicitly cited when taking UTRGV. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_UTSA_Roadrunners_football_team ; https://texasfootball.com/article/2023/07/25/bush-sees-similarities-and-differences-between-utsa-and-utrgv
- Houston (Levine, 2012–14): tempo spread with Air Raid influence (Meacham co-OC 2013); rewarded high-volume WRs (Greenberry 76 rec) and a freshman QB throwing 28 TD; the 2014 Wildcat/Ward package shows willingness to play a converted athlete at QB. https://uhcougars.com/news/2013/1/14/Doug_Meacham_Named_Houston_Offensive_Coordinator ; https://thedailycougar.com/2014/09/11/insertion-ward-wildcat-package-takes-pressure-okorn/
- Buffalo (Ryan/Roman/Lynn, 2015): run-first, two-back "balance in the backfield" with elite broken-tackle backs (McCoy, Williams 5.8 ypc); his one exposure to a power-run NFL system. https://www.pff.com/news/pro-bills-find-balance-in-backfield-with-mccoy-and-williams
- High school (Seguin/Canyon): 52-ppg district champion at Seguin and a 3,000-yd/34-TD QB at Canyon who became a P4 signee, consistent with a QB-centric spread. https://n.rivals.com/news/louisville-lands-central-texas-playmaker-qb-deuce-adams
- UTRGV 2025 synthesizes it: portal QB (Newcomer of the Year) + freshman WR (Freshman of the Year) + freshman OL All-American; 9-3 and sixth in Eddie Robinson voting, and he added a Big 12 assistant (Aych) as co-OC. https://www.southland.org/news/2025/11/25/southland-announces-2025-all-conference-football-team-and-superlatives.aspx ; https://www.footballscoop.com/2026/01/15/sources-emerging-fcs-power-lands-big-12-assistant
